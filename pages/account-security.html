GTA V Account Security & Takeover Guide

When taking ownership of a custom or pre-modded GTA V account, securing it is your absolute first priority. If you do not change the core credentials immediately, your account remains vulnerable to automated recovery systems, security breaches or unauthorized access.

Follow this step-by-step checklist to ensure total ownership whilst locking anyone else out.


Step One: Securing the Platform Account (Xbox/Microsoft)

Because your GTA character is tied directly to your console gamertag, you must secure the Microsoft Account First.

  • Change the Password Immediately: Log into the Microsoft Account Dashboard. Chose a strong, completely unique password that you do not use anywhere else.

  • Update Recovery Contact Info: Remove any pre-existing recovery email addresses or phone numbers. Link your own active email and mobile number.

  • Turn On Two-Step Verification (2FA): 1. Navigate to security settings > Two-Step Verification. 2. Set it to use an authenticator app (like Microsoft or Google Authenticator) or your phone number. 3. This ensures nobody can log into your gamertag without a code generated straight from your phone.

    Even if you log in via your console, the underlying data passes through Rockstar's systems. You must secure this separate profile.

  • Change the Linked Email:Log into the Rockstar Social Club website using the account credentials. Navigate to Settings > Profile and update the email to your personal address. You will need to confirm this change via your inbox.

  • Change the Password: Update your password to a strong phrase that differs from your Microsoft account password.

  • Check Linked Accounts: Go to your Linked Accounts tab. Ensure your current Xbox gamertag is connected, and immediately unlink any unfamiliar third-party platforms (like PlayStation, Epic Games, or Steam accounts) that shouldn't be there.

  • Enable Rockstar's 2FA: 1. Go to Settings > Two-Step Verification. 2. Scan the QR code using your preferred authenticator app. 3. Enter the verification code to activate it.
